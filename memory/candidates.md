# Candidate Cities (ranked)

All candidates are under Policía Nacional jurisdiction (a requirement; see registry L1 context in private memory). Rankings reflect the owner's values-first framework, not generic livability indices.

## 1. Gijón / Asturias
- Temperate Atlantic climate, no sustained heat. Critical for sensory comfort.
- Lowest expat density and lowest cost of living of all candidates.
- Cimadevilla (historic quarter) meets the aesthetic-coherence bar; Somió offers villas with gardens and sea views for the cats.
- Seasonal validity: July/August is peak Camino del Norte + domestic tourism; NOT representative. Best authentic-signal month: May. Prior planning set May 2027 as the evaluation window.

## 2. Málaga
- Mediterranean, newer housing stock, functional AC, growing tech/nomad community. Soho has strong aesthetic quality.
- Seasonal validity: July/August is 35 to 38°C and peak tourist season; worst possible livability signal for a heat-averse evaluator. Loving it despite the heat would be strong signal, but the default read is distorted.

## 3. Valencia
- Lower cost than Málaga, Ruzafa has genuine aesthetic coherence, larger remote-work infrastructure.
- Rising expat density (150k+ foreign residents, ~18.5% of city) is a standing concern; summer amplifies it.
- Seasonal validity: July/August 30 to 35°C and crowded. Confirmed best evaluation month: October.

## 4. Sevilla
- Strong civic identity, architectural beauty, cheaper than Málaga/Valencia.
- Seasonal validity: 38 to 42°C in July/August, among the hottest cities in Europe. Effectively disqualified for a summer evaluation trip.

## Separately tracked: Las Palmas de Gran Canaria
- Year-round ~22 to 24°C; NO seasonal distortion, a July visit equals an October visit for signal quality.
- Cheaper than mainland; requires a positioning leg (MAD or LIS → LPA), hard to combine with mainland cities inside 10 days.
- Key unknown: island containment vs island freedom; only ground time resolves it.
- The designed stress test: work actual Seattle hours (9am to 2pm Seattle = 5pm to 10pm local) for at least 3 days.
- Prior planning flagged late October (Oct 25+) as the optimal window for timezone-overlap testing (7-hour gap).
